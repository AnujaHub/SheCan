Who we are

About SheCan Foundation

She Can Foundation is a government-registered NGO dedicated to empowering women through education, healthcare awareness, skill development, and
economic opportunities. We work to create a more equitable society where every woman can achieve her full potential.

Women Empowerment

Supporting women through awareness, opportunities, and community-driven initiatives.

Menstrual Health

Promoting menstrual hygiene awareness and helping girls access essential sanitary products.

Community Impact

Working with volunteers and supporters to create lasting change across communities.




“Together, we can break down barriers and empower women. At She Can Foundation, we believe that if we all do our part, there is no challenge too great to overcome. Join us in our mission to create a world where every woman has the opportunity to thrive and succeed.”

— Reeta Mishra

Founder & President, She Can Foundation
